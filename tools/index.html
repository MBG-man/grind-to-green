Frequently Asked Questions

What free finance tools does GrindToGreen offer?

We currently offer a compound interest calculator and a budget tracker. Both are free, work in your browser, and require no installation. We are actively developing more tools based on community feedback.

Do I need to sign up to use these tools?

No sign-up is required to use the compound interest calculator. The budget tracker works fully offline in your browser. Optional cloud sync is available if you create a free account.

Are these financial tools accurate?

Yes, the calculators use standard financial formulas and are updated regularly. They are designed for educational and planning purposes. Always consult a qualified financial advisor for personalized advice.

Can I use these tools on my phone?

Yes. All tools are fully responsive and work on mobile, tablet, and desktop. You can also install them as a web app on your home screen for quick offline access.

How do I save my calculations?

The compound interest calculator saves your last inputs locally in your browser. You can also export results as PDF or CSV. The budget tracker can sync your data across devices when you sign up for a free account.
